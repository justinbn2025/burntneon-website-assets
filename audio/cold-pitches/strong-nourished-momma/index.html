What to listen for

  • Your voice stays at one steady level, so nobody reaches for the volume.
  • Less of the room, so you sound closer and more present.
  • Softer “s” sounds, which are easier on earbuds.
  • A cleaner, less boxy tone overall.

Headphones or earbuds show the difference best.
